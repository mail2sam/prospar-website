About Prospar

We manage wealth the way wealth is actually built: slowly, deliberately, and with the evidence on our side.

How we think

Most investment outcomes in India are decided long before any product is chosen. They are decided by whether a family starts early or late; whether the SIP survives its third flat year; whether idle money is left to inflation; whether last year's winning fund is chased into next year's disappointment. The data on this is unambiguous: we've published much of it in our Insights, and it shapes everything we do.

So we begin where the evidence says returns are actually made: behaviour and structure. A plan you can hold through a 30% drawdown beats a brilliant strategy you'll abandon. An emergency fund, clean insurance, and goal-matched horizons come before any conversation about alpha. Once that foundation exists, sophistication has a place, diversified funds first, and for qualified investors, professionally managed PMS and AIF strategies selected with the same discipline.

We are candid by policy. Our illustrations use conservative assumptions and state them. Our calculators show the uncomfortable numbers (total interest on your home loan, the real value of your pension in today's rupees) because trust built on honest arithmetic is the only kind that survives a market cycle.

How we work

  • Education before products. You'll understand the reasoning (and the risks) before you're asked to commit a rupee.
  • Structure before selection. Foundation first; growth assets matched to goals and horizons; sophistication only where it genuinely adds.
  • Full disclosure. We are compensated through distribution arrangements, and we tell you exactly how. No hidden economics.
  • Partner attention. As a boutique LLP we carry no sales targets and no product-of-the-month. You deal with partners, not a relationship-manager queue.
