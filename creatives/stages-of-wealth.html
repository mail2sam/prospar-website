A map, not a race
Which rung of the wealth ladder are you on?
0
Dependent
Debt payments are bigger than your own income
1
Solvency
You meet every payment from your own income
2
Stability
Emergency fund ready · high-cost debt cleared
3
Security
Assets cover 5–10 years of your expenses
4
Independence
Income from assets covers today's lifestyle
5
Freedom
Asset income funds the lifestyle and the bigger goals
6
Abundance
More cash flow from assets than you will ever need
Every rung has a plan: emergency fund → SIP → step-up → diversified growth → income for life. Climbing is a habit, not an event.
Framework: the widely used "stages of financial wealth" model, presented by Prospar. For education only — not investment advice. Investments are subject to market risks.
