A working theory of wealth
You hold three currencies:
knowledge, time and money.
K
Knowledge
Compounds the fastest. Costs only attention. Nobody can take it back.
T
Time
The only currency you cannot buy more of — every year unspent on compounding is gone.
M
Money
The one that can buy back the other two — but only once it is invested, not parked.
Early on, trade time and knowledge for money. Later, money buys back your time. Wealth is managing the exchange rate.
