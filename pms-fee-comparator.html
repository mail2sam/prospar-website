Fees paid over your holding period

Each structure's fee is deducted from the portfolio every year, so later years compound on a smaller base: the quiet cost of fees is the growth they never earn.

Assumptions

This comparator illustrates fee mechanics only; it is not investment advice, a forecast of returns, or an offer of any product. Fee menus, hurdle definitions, high-water mark and catch-up wording vary by provider and are governed solely by the final PMS agreement: always confirm the current schedule in writing before signing.
