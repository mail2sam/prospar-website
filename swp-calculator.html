Assumptions

This calculator is for illustration and education only. It assumes a constant rate of return; market-linked corpora move with markets, and poor early returns (sequence risk) can exhaust a corpus sooner than a constant-return projection suggests. Taxes on redemptions are not modelled. This is not investment advice. Prospar Consulting LLP: investments are subject to market risks; read all scheme-related documents carefully.
