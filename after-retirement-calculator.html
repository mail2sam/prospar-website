Assumptions

This check is for illustration and education only. It assumes constant returns and steady inflation; real markets vary year to year, and poor returns early in retirement (sequence risk) can deplete a corpus faster. Taxes and medical or other one-time shocks are not modelled. This is not investment advice. Prospar Consulting LLP: investments are subject to market risks.
