Assumptions

This calculator provides an educational estimate for resident individuals under the Income-tax law as amended to date; it is not tax advice and not a substitute for a chartered accountant or the official portal at the time of filing. Cess, surcharge with approximated marginal relief, the ₹1.25 lakh equity LTCG exemption and Section 87A rebates are applied; TDS, advance-tax interest, business income, foreign assets, set-off carry-forwards and the pre-July-2024 property indexation option are outside its scope.
